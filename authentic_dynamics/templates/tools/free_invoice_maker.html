How totals are calculated

Round quantity × rate to the currency’s minor unit, then apply each item discount. Allocate the invoice discount proportionally across those net items. Add charges, then calculate each tax independently on discounted taxable items plus taxable charges. Round each tax; add totals; subtract payments and credits. Taxes do not compound. Negative inputs are rejected; use payments/credits instead. JPY uses whole yen; other supported currencies use two decimals.

How to create an invoice

Enter your business and customer details, choose an invoice number and dates, and add goods or services with quantities and rates. Review discounts, taxes and any deposits, then add payment instructions. Check the preview before downloading or printing. Save locally and export a JSON backup if you want to edit it on another device.

What should an invoice include?

A useful invoice identifies the seller and customer, an invoice number, issue and due dates, descriptions, quantities, rates, subtotal, applicable taxes, total and payment terms. Requirements vary by location and business; check which identifiers and wording apply to you.

Invoice vs receipt

An invoice sets out an amount requested for goods or services. A receipt acknowledges a payment received. Recording a payment here adjusts the balance, but this tool does not independently verify that money was received.

Invoice vs estimate

An estimate describes an expected price before work is finalized. An invoice requests payment for the agreed goods or services. This tool creates invoices; confirm the scope and price with your customer.

What does Net 30 mean?

Net 30 usually means payment is due 30 days after the invoice date. This tool adds 30 calendar days. You can select other terms or override the due date to match your agreement.

How should invoice numbers work?

A consistent sequence such as INV-001, INV-002 helps you find and reference invoices. This tool suggests the next INV number from your local library, but you can edit it. It does not coordinate numbers across browsers, devices or other systems.

Do I need to charge sales tax?

That depends on your jurisdiction, transaction and circumstances. Enter only rates and taxability you have verified with the relevant authority or a qualified adviser. This calculator does not determine or file your taxes.
