Your video is processed directly in your browser. It is not uploaded to Authentic Dynamics or sent to a third-party conversion service. No account or conversion history.

How it works
  1. You select a video from your device.
  2. Your browser reads the file locally.
  3. Your device performs the conversion.
  4. Download saves the converted file back to your device.

The first conversion downloads an approximately 31 MB engine from this website. Only website assets travel over the network. Videos and results stay in temporary browser memory until cleared or the page closes.

Give your device a moment. Keep this tab open and your device awake. Conversion can take longer than the video itself. Mobile browsers may run out of memory or pause background tabs.

Compatibility. Requires JavaScript, WebAssembly and Web Workers. Common MP4, WebM and MOV files are supported by the conversion engine, but some codecs or damaged files may fail. A video that cannot preview may still convert. MP4 uses H.264/AAC; WebM uses VP8/Opus. Extract audio needs an audio track.
