Time & labor

For employees, include wages plus payroll taxes and benefits. For your own time, enter the hourly amount you intend to earn. Leaving labor at $0 treats it as free.

Other job costs

Include job-specific travel, rentals or disposal in the travel field. Overhead is your share of recurring costs such as insurance, software, rent or vehicle expenses. Count each cost only once.

Your target

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price, not a markup on cost. This calculator applies your target to all entered costs, including allocated overhead. Use 0% to cover costs without adding profit.

BUILD A BETTER ESTIMATE

Price the work.
Not just the parts.

Count your own time. A job can look profitable when you leave out the hours you spend on it. Enter a realistic loaded labor cost, even when you are the person doing the work.

Margin is not markup. If your entered costs are $650 and your target margin is 35%, the price is $1,000: $350 remains after those costs. Adding 35% to $650 would produce a different result.

Make overhead visible. Allocate a fair share of recurring business costs to the job. The calculator applies your target margin after that allocation, so avoid counting the same cost twice.

No local price guesswork. Sample numbers are illustrative, not Central PA market rates. Replace them with your actual costs, scope and experience.

Know what is outside the estimate. Sales tax, income tax, permit fees and costs you do not enter are not calculated. Verify which charges apply before sending a customer a final price.

When the scope and price are agreed, create an invoice with your business details and payment terms.
