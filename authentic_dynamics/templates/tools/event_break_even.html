PLAN THE DAY

Enough sales.
Enough margin.

Contribution is what one sale leaves behind. Average sale − product cost − percentage fees = contribution. That money first covers your fixed event costs, then contributes to profit.

Break-even transactions = fixed costs ÷ contribution per sale, rounded up. For a profit goal, add that goal to fixed costs before dividing. If contribution is zero or negative, more sales cannot produce a positive profit.

Try a slower day. Lower expected transactions and recalculate. Compare the required sales with how many customers you can realistically serve during the event.

An estimate, not a sales forecast. Results assume every transaction has the same average value and cost. Percentage fees are rounded to cents per transaction. Include spoilage or unsold stock costs where relevant. Results exclude taxes on profit and overhead you have not entered.
