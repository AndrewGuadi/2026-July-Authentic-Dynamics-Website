GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we build.

What does the $500 starting price cover?+

A focused mobile-first business site with essential information, a contact path, local search basics, analytics setup and first-year standard hosting. We confirm exact pages, content and revisions in the quote.

Can you improve an existing website?+

Yes. We start by seeing what works and what gets in the way, then recommend targeted changes or a rebuild where it makes sense.

Will I own my site?+

Your domain, content and agreed project assets are yours under the project agreement. Third-party tools keep their own license terms.

What happens after launch?+

You receive a handoff and can choose ongoing hosting, maintenance or growth support. Those services and renewal costs are agreed separately.
