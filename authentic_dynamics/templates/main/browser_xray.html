11 / MYTHS & BOUNDARIES

Powerful doesn’t
mean all-seeing.

A normal webpage operating through standard browser security controls does not automatically get:

  • Your text messages or call history
  • Your passwords
  • Every file or your private photo library
  • Private data from other apps
  • Your camera or microphone without authorization
  • Your precise location without authorization
  • Your whole screen without an explicit sharing action

Choosing a file, saved permissions, browser extensions, enterprise integrations, installed software, compromised devices and browser vulnerabilities can change the situation. These are boundaries of ordinary webpages, not guarantees about every piece of software.
