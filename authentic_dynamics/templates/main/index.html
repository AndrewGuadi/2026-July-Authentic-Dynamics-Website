A FEW THINGS WORTH ASKING

Straight
answers.

Do I really own my website?+

Yes. Ownership of your agreed code, content and project assets is set out in your scope and handoff. Your domain and business accounts should be under your control. Third-party software remains subject to its own licenses.

What does a website starting at $500 include?+

A focused business presence with mobile-first design, business and service information, a contact or booking inquiry path, basic local SEO, analytics, social links, domain connection and standard first-year hosting. We confirm the page count, content responsibilities and revisions in your quote. Advanced features and ongoing costs are scoped separately.

Can you improve the site I already have?+

Yes. We start by looking at what is working and what is getting in the way. That may lead to focused updates, better local search, a clearer inquiry flow or a full rebuild when it makes sense.

Are the community tools actually free?+

Yes. These tools run in your browser without an account. They create planning outputs using your answers; they don’t scan Google, audit your website or use AI. Inputs aren’t sent to us. You can download a result and choose whether to share it.

Do you only work with Central PA businesses?+

Central Pennsylvania is our home and community focus. We also work with organizations elsewhere when the project is a good fit.

What happens after I reach out?+

We talk through your goals, answer your questions and determine what kind of help makes sense. Any proposed project comes with a clear scope and price before work begins.
